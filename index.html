GaiaWM

Exploring hybrid world models for intelligent agents — dynamic, contextual, and extensible.

What is GaiaWM?

GaiaWM is an experimental project aimed at building a next-generation world model for autonomous reasoning agents. It blends spatial context, evolving knowledge, and configurable world data to enable agents to perceive, act, and adapt in rich environments.

Inspired by Open Mapping Initiatives

The project relies on data from open mapping efforts like OpenHistoryMap, a crowd-driven historical atlas capturing changes in geography over time, and creative cartography platforms like OpenFantasyMap, which bring fictional worlds to life. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

While those maps focus on rendering data about real and imagined worlds, GaiaWM is focused on the *representation of world knowledge in a structured form agents can query, reason with, and interact against* — a different slice of the same spatial imagination.

Early Features

  • Configurable world definitions (objects, terrain, agents).
  • RESTful access to localized environmental context.
  • Designed to support cognitive agent loops with perception and action.
  • Extensible towards both historical, fictional, and hybrid spaces.
