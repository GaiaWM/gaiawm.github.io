GaiaWM

A new approach to world models — where context, perception, and interaction matter as much as geography.

What GaiaWM Is

**GaiaWM** isn’t just a bigger map — it’s a **contextual world model** for intelligent agents. Instead of assuming perfect global knowledge, GaiaWM models *what an agent believes and can act on*, given partial perception, dynamic interpretation, and interaction with others.

Key Concepts

  • Perception & Belief — Agents perceive slices of the world and form internal beliefs, not total knowledge.
  • Affordances — What objects and places *enable* an agent to do, based on interpretation, not fixed categories.
  • Social Interaction — Agents can communicate, share interpretations, negotiate meaning, and update each other’s beliefs.
  • Contextual Narrative — World interpretation goes beyond geometry: narrative summaries, threats, opportunities, and implicit meaning.

Eye on Fantasy Worlds

One of GaiaWM’s first experiences is the “Eye on Fantasy Worlds” feature — a narrative lens into the world. When you explore a region on OpenFantasyMaps, GaiaWM can generate a contextual narration of that slice of the world:

  • What landmarks or features are present
  • How they relate to each other
  • Potential threats or opportunities for agents
  • Cultural or environmental mood of the locale

This transforms a static map into meaningful, interpretable place — perfect for worldbuilding, narrative AI, and immersive systems.

Multi-Agent Communication

GaiaWM now supports **agent-to-agent communication** — so agents don’t just act independently; they can:

  • Share context and interpretations
  • Exchange meaningful observations
  • Negotiate plans and coordinate goals
  • Build shared understanding over time

This goes beyond traditional social simulacra models by enabling *explicit exchange of semantic information*, not merely inference from observed behavior.
